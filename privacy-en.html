2. In short

The App does not collect personal data, does not send anything to a server and does not share anything with third parties. You don't need an account. There are no ads, analytics or tracking tools.

3. What stays on your device

Your level, score, star count, play statistics (hints used, total play time) and settings (theme, sound, vibration, color-blind mode) are stored only on your own device. We never receive them. Deleting the App deletes all of it.

4. In-app purchases

When you buy a star pack, the payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Your payment details are never shared with us. The App only learns that a purchase was completed and which pack it was, and adds the stars. These purchases happen under your agreement with the store, and Apple's and Google's privacy policies apply to them. No personal data is transferred to us in the process.

5. Internet access

You don't need the internet to play. Your device only contacts Apple's or Google's store services when you open the shop or make a purchase. Fonts and sounds are built into the App and are never loaded from outside servers.

6. Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. You can restrict in-app purchases with your device's parental controls.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, data protection laws such as the GDPR (EU/UK) or KVKK (Türkiye) give you rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because the App does not process personal data, we don't hold any data about you. You can still contact us at aykutandroid@gmail.com with any request, and we will reply within 30 days, free of charge.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and in the App, and the date above will be updated.
